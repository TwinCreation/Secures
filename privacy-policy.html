Your messages and contacts stay on your device.

Messages: SMS Organizer & MMS does not upload, sell, or share your text messages, phone numbers, or contacts with us or anyone else. There are no ads and no analytics that read your message content.

1. About this policy

This policy explains how Messages: SMS Organizer & MMS ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use it on your Android device. The app is an SMS and MMS messaging app that can be set as your default messaging app. It shows your conversations, sends and receives messages, and helps you keep them organized.

By installing and using the app, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please uninstall the app.

2. What the app accesses

To work as a messaging app, the app reads and handles the following on your device only:

  • Text and multimedia messages: message content, sender and recipient phone numbers, dates, and delivery status, so it can show your conversations and let you send and receive messages.
  • Contacts: contact display names, phone numbers, and starred status, so it can show names instead of raw numbers and let you pick a contact when starting a new message. Other contact details such as email addresses, postal addresses, and notes are not accessed.
  • SIM information: which SIM slot is active, carrier name, and SIM subscription ID, so you can choose which SIM sends a message on dual-SIM phones. The app does not access your device IMEI or IMSI, and it does not read your call status or call history.
  • Your own phone number (if stored on the SIM): used only to label your SIMs on screen.

This information is processed on your device. We do not receive it.

3. Permissions and why we ask

Android asks for your approval before the app can use sensitive features. Here is what each permission is used for.

Permission What it is used for
READ_SMS Read your messages from the system inbox to display conversations.
RECEIVE_SMS Receive new text messages as they arrive.
SEND_SMS Send text messages to the recipients you choose.
WRITE_SMS Update the system message store, for example marking messages as read or deleting a conversation.
RECEIVE_MMS, RECEIVE_WAP_PUSH Receive picture and multimedia message alerts. Android requires these for any default messaging app.
READ_CONTACTS Show contact names and initials, and let you choose contacts for new messages.
READ_PHONE_STATE, READ_PHONE_NUMBERS Identify your SIM cards so you can pick which one sends a message.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Show notifications for new messages, with quick actions such as Mark as read, Copy OTP, and Reply.
VIBRATE Vibrate when a message arrives.
INTERNET Basic network access used for app integrity checks (see Internet use). Your messages and contacts are not sent over it.

If you decline the contacts permission, the app still works and simply shows phone numbers instead of names.

4. Where your data is stored

Your messages stay in Android's own message storage on your phone. To make the app fast and available offline, it also keeps a local copy (a cache) of your messages and contacts in a private database inside the app's protected storage. Android restricts that storage so other apps cannot read it.

  • Retention: the local copy is kept while the app is installed. If a message is deleted from the system message store, the app removes its copy too.
  • Uninstalling: removing the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, erases everything the app stored.
  • Device backups: whether app data is included in Android's backup follows the backup rules set in the app and your device's own backup settings.

5. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your messages, phone numbers, or contacts with third parties. We do not collect this data on any server, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

When you send a text or picture message, it travels through your mobile carrier to the recipient, the same as with any messaging app. Your carrier's own privacy policy applies to that delivery.

6. Internet use

The app requests network access only for standard app integrity checks (Firebase App Check), which help confirm that requests come from a genuine copy of the app. These checks do not read or transmit your messages or contacts. As with most apps that use Google services, Google may process limited technical information, such as device and app signals, to provide these checks, under Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Your choices and deletion

  • Permissions: you can grant or revoke any permission at any time in Android Settings > Apps > Messages: SMS Organizer & MMS > Permissions. Some features will stop working without the related permission.
  • Delete messages: delete a message or conversation in the app to remove it from your device.
  • Delete everything: clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app to erase all data it holds.
  • Default app: you can switch your default messaging app in Android settings whenever you like.

Because we do not collect your data on our servers, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

8. Security

Your data stays inside the app's private storage on your device, protected by Android's app sandbox. Components that receive messages are locked so only the Android system can trigger them, which prevents other apps from injecting fake messages. Keeping your phone updated and protected with a screen lock also helps keep your messages safe. No method of storage is completely risk-free, but we design the app to keep your data on your device and out of our hands.

9. Children's privacy

The app is a general messaging tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because message and contact data never leaves the device.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when the app adds a feature. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it last changed. We encourage you to review it occasionally.
